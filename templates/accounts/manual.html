📖 1. Overview

CourseCart is an online platform that connects Tanzanian youth with affordable vocational skills training. Students can enroll in courses like Plumbing, Electrical Installation, Tailoring, Carpentry, Welding, and more.

Courses include video lessons, PDF documents, and certificates upon completion — all accessible from any device.

🚀 2. Getting Started

Create an Account

  1. Visit the CourseCart website
  2. Click "Get Started" or "Register as Student"
  3. Fill in your full name, email, and password
  4. Choose your role: Student or Instructor
  5. Click "Create Account"

Login

  1. Click "Login" on the navigation bar
  2. Enter your email and password
  3. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

👥 3. User Roles

🎓 Student

Browse courses, enroll, pay, learn, and earn certificates

👨‍🏫 Instructor

Create courses, add lessons, manage students, issue certificates

🎓 4. Student Guide

Browse Courses

  1. Click "Browse Courses" in the navigation bar
  2. Use the search bar or category filters to find courses
  3. Click on a course card to view details

Enroll in a Course

  1. On the course detail page, click "Enroll Now"
  2. If the course is free, you'll be enrolled immediately
  3. If paid, you'll be redirected to the payment page

Complete Payment

  1. Select your payment method (Mobile Money, Bank Card, or Cash)
  2. Enter required details and click "Pay TZS [amount]"
  3. Wait for the simulated payment to process
  4. On success, you'll have access to all lessons

Access Lessons

  1. Go to "My Courses" from your dashboard
  2. Click "Continue" on the course
  3. Watch videos, read PDFs, and study notes
  4. Click "Mark Complete" after finishing each lesson

Track Progress

Your dashboard shows active courses with progress percentages. A completion bar fills as you finish lessons. When you reach 100%, you've completed the course!

💡 Tip: Download PDF documents for offline study using the "Download PDF" button on lesson pages.

👨‍🏫 5. Instructor Guide

Create a Course

  1. From your dashboard, click "Create New Course"
  2. Fill in the course title, category, description, and price
  3. Upload a thumbnail image for your course
  4. Set duration, level, and certificate availability
  5. Click "Publish Course"

Add Lessons

  1. Go to "My Courses" and click "Manage" on a course
  2. Click "Add Lesson"
  3. Choose lesson type: YouTube Video, PDF Document, Upload Video, or Text/Notes
  4. Fill in the lesson title, content, and duration
  5. Click "Add Lesson"

Manage Students

  1. On the course management page, view enrolled students
  2. Track their progress and completion status
  3. Certificates are issued automatically upon completion
💡 Tip: Use YouTube videos for easy hosting. Upload PDF notes for supplementary reading material. Set the first lesson as "Free Preview" to attract students.

💰 6. Payments

CourseCart supports three payment methods:

  • 📱 Mobile Money — M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money
  • 💳 Bank Card — Visa, Mastercard
  • 💵 Cash Payment — Pay at authorized agents

All payments are simulated for testing. In production, real payment gateways will be integrated.

View your payment history from the sidebar under "Payment History".

🔔 7. Notifications

You'll receive notifications for:

  • ✅ Successful payments and enrollments
  • 📚 New enrollments (for instructors)
  • 📜 Certificate issuance
  • ⚠️ Payment failures

Click the 🔔 bell icon in the navigation bar to view all notifications. Unread notifications are highlighted with a blue dot.
